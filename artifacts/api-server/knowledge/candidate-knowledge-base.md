## 2. Who Is Aliu Johnson Temitope?

Aliu Johnson Temitope is a member of the Patrician Set of 2012 of St. Patrick's College, Iwaro-Oka, and is seeking the mandate of his classmates to serve as President of the set. He is a Geography and Planning Sciences graduate who works as Head of Data and Research at Fortuna Technologies LLC. He lives in Abuja and is single.

He is interested in research, technology, data-driven decision-making and community development. His interests include data analysis, GIS, web development, robotics, artificial intelligence, automation, the Internet of Things (IoT), civic technology and sustainable development.

He grew up with the conviction that how you do anything is how you do everything. Colleagues who have worked closely with him describe his strong work ethic, resilience, empathy and refusal to give up when circumstances become difficult.

## 11. Vision, Goals and Plan

### Plan 1: Technology, Innovation and the Next Generation (Project Patrician FutureTech)

Johnson believes the set has a responsibility to prepare the next generation for an increasingly technology-driven world. Under **Project Patrician FutureTech**, his administration will:

- Organise practical training for students of St. Patrick's College in **Computer Vision** and **Large Language Models (LLMs)**.
- Hold a **Hackathon** at the end of the training, where students build solutions to real-world problems.
- Award **cash prizes** to outstanding participants.
- Goal: inspire students to become creators of solutions, not merely consumers of technology.

### Plan 2: Members' Welfare and Collective Responsibility (Set 2012 Welfare Fund)

The association should be a community where members can depend on one another. His administration will:

- Establish a **Set 2012 Welfare Fund**, sustained by agreed monthly contributions.
- Provide financial support to members during weddings, bereavement, illness, medical emergencies and other exceptional circumstances.
- Put clear guidelines and transparent financial reporting in place.
- Goal: build a sustainable support system so that no member feels forgotten when they need the set most.

### Plan 3: Inclusive Leadership and Collective Participation

Leadership should create opportunities for everyone to contribute. His administration will:

- Set up functional committees: welfare, finance, education, technology, publicity and others.
- Encourage members to join according to their interests, professional backgrounds and capacity.
- Make the set's WhatsApp platform more engaging and participatory, so members have a voice in decisions affecting the set.
- Principle: the presidency is a collective effort, not a one-man show.

### Plan 4: Digital Identity and Institutional Memory (Set 2012 Website)

His administration will build a dedicated website for the Patrician Set of 2012 to serve as its digital home and archive. The website will:

- Document the set's activities and preserve its memories.
- Showcase members' achievements and businesses.
- Provide updates on developmental projects.
- Offer a newsletter subscription so students, alumni and interested stakeholders can follow the set's activities.
- Goal: give our history a proper home instead of scattered photographs and disappearing WhatsApp conversations.
